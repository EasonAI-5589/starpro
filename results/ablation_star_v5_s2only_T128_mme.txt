Ablation Study Purpose & Schedule Comparison
================================================================================

This experiment isolates the contribution of Stage 2 (Progressive Pruning)
by skipping Stage 1 (THCP).

Schedule Evolution:
1. **Uniform Schedule** (SparseVLM-aligned):
   - Schedule: [(2, 320), (5, 128), (9, 64)]
   - Distribution: Early pruning, uniform reduction
   - MME Score: 1400.5

2. **Front-Heavy Schedule** (Current):
   - Schedule: [(2, 192), (12, 64), (24, 24)]
   - Distribution: Keep more tokens in first 14 layers, aggressive after layer 14
   - MME Score: 1681.6 ✨ (+281.1 points, +20.1%)

Key Finding: Front-heavy schedule significantly improves performance!
  → Early layers need more visual tokens for semantic understanding
  → Later layers can work with fewer tokens for reasoning

Comparison for Table 6:
- S1 Only (THCP):     ~98.0% retention @ T=128
- S2 Only (front-heavy): MME = 1681.6
- S1+S2 (STAR-Pro):   ~98.4% retention @ T=128
