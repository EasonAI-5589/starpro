================================================================================
Ablation Study Purpose
================================================================================

This experiment isolates the contribution of Stage 2 (Progressive Pruning)
by skipping Stage 1 (THCP) on text-based visual question answering.

TextVQA is particularly challenging as it requires:
  - Reading and understanding text in images (OCR capability)
  - Spatial reasoning about text placement
  - Answering questions based on visual text content

Comparison for Table 6:

- S2 Only (front-heavy):   52.73%

Analysis:
  - S2 Only achieves 52.73% on TextVQA with front-heavy schedule
  - TextVQA requires fine-grained visual details (text recognition)
  - Front-heavy schedule helps preserve text details in early layers
  - 128 avg tokens (77.8% reduction) maintains reasonable text reading ability

Note: TextVQA is inherently challenging for pruned models as OCR requires
      high-resolution visual features. The 52.73% result shows that even
      with aggressive pruning (to 128 avg tokens), the model retains
      substantial text comprehension capability.
